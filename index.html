Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How many days are enough for Sikkim?

Five to seven days is a good starting point for a first trip. We can extend the route for North or West Sikkim.

Can I customize the package?

Yes. Duration, hotels, destinations, vehicle type and sightseeing can be customized.

Is Nathula Pass included?

It can be included in eligible itineraries, subject to permits and weather conditions.

Do you arrange airport transfers?

Yes. Private transfers from Bagdogra airport and NJP can be included in your itinerary.

Is North Sikkim safe for tourists?

Your plan is built around current road conditions, permits and local travel restrictions.

Can senior citizens travel?

Yes, with appropriate pacing and consideration of altitude and road conditions.
